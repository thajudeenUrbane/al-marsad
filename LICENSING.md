Generated pages, such as `demo/index.html`, combine code and data. Each part keeps its own licence.

## What this means in practice

- **Code (AGPL):** you may run, copy and change it. If you share a changed version, or run one as a website other people use, you must publish your source code under the same licence.
- **Data and writing (CC BY-SA):** you may copy, adapt and reuse them, commercially too, as long as you credit al-Marṣad and release what you build from them under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence.
- **Identifier tables (CC0):** no conditions. They are in the public domain so that other databases, Wikidata among them, can link to al-Marṣad IDs.
