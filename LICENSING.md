Generated pages, such as `demo/index.html`, combine code and data. Each part keeps its own licence.

Third-party data: rows of `ontology/corpus/external-ids.csv` and `external-coordinates.csv` whose source is al-Thurayya keep al-Thurayya's own licence, CC BY 4.0 (credit: the al-Thurayya Project, althurayya.github.io); the rest of those files is CC0. `site/interactive/data/basemap.json` is simplified from Natural Earth land outlines, which are in the public domain (naturalearthdata.com); the file records its source and checksum.

Ibn Hishām's *al-Sīra al-Nabawiyya* (slice 4): `ontology/corpus/ibn-hisham.ttl` holds only pointers into the text (page, milestone, line and character offsets, and checksums), personal, place and event names, ascriptions and chains as the text writes them, and short age and date expressions, taken from the OpenITI text `0213IbnHisham.SiraNabawiyya.Shamela0023833-ara1` (OpenITI, github.com/OpenITI/0225AH; text from al-Shamela; base edition al-Saqqā, al-Abyārī and Shalabī, 2nd ed., Cairo 1375/1955, as the file's metadata names it, provisional). OpenITI releases are licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, and that text is **not redistributed here**: it is not committed, the folder it is fetched into (`ingest/cache/`) is gitignored, and `ingest/ibn_hisham.py fetch` downloads it from OpenITI at a pinned commit and checks its checksum. The claims' glosses are machine-written summaries, labelled as such, not a translation. Credit: OpenITI and the edition above. The maintainer has yet to confirm that quoting names and these short expressions is acceptable (see [docs/handover/slice-4.md](docs/handover/slice-4.md) and [docs/handover/slice-4-int.md](docs/handover/slice-4-int.md)).

## What this means in practice

- **Code (AGPL):** you may run, copy and change it. If you share a changed version, or run one as a website other people use, you must publish your source code under the same licence.
- **Data and writing (CC BY-SA):** you may copy, adapt and reuse them, commercially too, as long as you credit al-Marṣad and release what you build from them under CC BY-SA 4.0 or a compatible licence.
- **Identifier tables (CC0):** no conditions. They are in the public domain so that other databases, Wikidata among them, can link to al-Marṣad IDs.
