Phase 0 · Research & architecture · September 2026

al-Marṣadالمرصد

A knowledge observatory for the Seerah and Islamic history — in which the atomic unit is not the historical fact, but the transmitted report with its chain.

Status architecture · ontology v0.1.0-draft Corpus 13 documents · 1,804 triples Prototype the Hijrah
00

The obvious way to build this is a knowledge graph of Islamic history with a 3D viewer on top and a chatbot beside it. That system is achievable, would demo well, and would be wrong in a way that could not be repaired later — because it would store settled facts and attach sources as a courtesy, and no amount of subsequent work can reconstruct an evidentiary structure that was never modelled.

This corpus argues for a different foundation, and the argument is not primarily technological.

The Islamic scholarly tradition already invented the data architecture this system requires — roughly a thousand years before computing.

Consider what the ḥadīth sciences are, described in the vocabulary of information architecture. The إسناد isnād is a provenance chain: an ordered, verifiable custody record for every claim. علم الرجال ʿilm al-rijāl is a prosopographical authority file resolving the identity of tens of thousands of transmitters. جرح وتعديل jarḥ wa taʿdīl is a reliability framework — per-source, per-evaluator, with reasoned justification. طبقات ṭabaqāt is generational stratification. إجازة ijāza is a credentialing model governing who may transmit what.

Systems that treat Islamic history as content to be digitized discard this apparatus and replace it with a footnote. That is a catastrophic loss of resolution — the equivalent of scanning a manuscript at 72 dpi.

The proposal inverts it. The apparatus is not decoration on a modern data model. The apparatus is the data model. What follows is an implementation of an Islamic epistemological system in a computational medium — one that modern digital humanities is still, slowly and independently, reinventing.

On the name al-Marṣad is the Arabic word for an astronomical observatory — Maragha, Samarkand, Istanbul. Proposed as both a name and a category. Subject to scholarly review; naming is the first decision that should leave engineering hands.

The architecture

Seven layers. Data flows up, queries flow down, and nothing skips a layer.

01

Two properties of this stack matter more than its contents. L3 is derived, not authored — the historical world is a projection of the evidence, rebuildable at any time, so changing a grading updates the world rather than requiring the history to be rewritten. And L6 cannot reach past L3 — the renderer holds no private data, no hardcoded routes, no decorative entities.

The inversion

The single decision from which everything else follows.

02

In the conventional model, a fact is stored and a citation is attached. The evidence cannot be queried, weighed or disagreed with, because it was never modelled as an object. Conflicting reports must either be reconciled by an editor or discarded, and confidence has nowhere to live but a badge.

Here the خبر khabar — the transmitted report with its chain — is primitive. Events, biographies and relationships are reconstructions derived from clusters of reports. The user still sees “the Hijrah occurred in Rabīʿ al-Awwal, 1 AH,” but that statement is now a projection over an evidentiary base that remains intact beneath it.

Four dimensions, rendered through three

The 4D promise is honest to the user. The model beneath it needs seven.

03
D1 EntityWhat existed — people, events, places, books, tribes, institutions, reports, concepts
D2 RelationHow it connected — and a connection is an object with its own time, place, evidence and disputes
D3 SpaceWhere — historical geography, routes, territories, and the uncertainty of location
D4 TimeWhen it changed — the network as a system with dynamics you can watch

The epistemic three

D5 EvidenceHow do we know — provenance as substrate, not annotation. Cannot be retrofitted.
D6 Record-timeWhen was it written — the historiographic axis. The most novel element in the architecture.
D7 InterpretationAccording to which reading — Sunni, Shia, Ibadi, academic. A system with one normative narrative cannot later become plural.

D6 deserves emphasis. Every historical system has event-time. Almost none treats source-time as navigable. But the Islamic sources are dense, dated and stratified — Ibn Isḥāq (d. 150/767), al-Wāqidī (d. 207/822), Ibn Saʿd (d. 230/845), al-Ṭabarī (d. 310/923).

Hold event-time fixed at the Hijrah and move record-time from 120 AH to 310 AH. Watch which details enter the tradition when, which chains proliferate, where later sources add specificity earlier ones lacked. That is not a visualization of history — it is a visualization of historiography, and it is a research instrument rather than a teaching toy.

Cost Storing record-time costs almost nothing at ingestion. Adding it later is impossible: it must be captured at the moment a report is bound to its source.

The isnād is four objects at once

One encoding, four capabilities. This is what makes the project's scope affordable.

04

A chain — A ← B ← C ← Companion ← the Prophet ﷺ — is simultaneously four things, and they are the same data.

As evidenceThe warrant for a claim; the object of ḥadīth criticism
As networkWho taught whom, who met whom, across generations
As geographyEach narrator has a city — the chain traces knowledge moving Madinah → Kufa → Nishapur → Cordoba
As chronologyEach narrator has dates — contact plausibility is checkable against them

Therefore the knowledge-transmission layer and the evidence layer are not two subsystems. They are one graph read two ways. “Is this report well-attested?” and “how did this teaching reach Malabar?” traverse the same edges. Every unit of scholarly effort spent encoding a chain pays into four capabilities at once — and the most expensive data to produce is also the most reusable.

Because every narrator carries dates and places, the system can continuously scan for chains whose adjacent narrators could not plausibly have met — the classical لقاء liqāʾ analysis, executed across the whole corpus. The system flags; it does not rule. A flagged chain is a question for a specialist, never an automated verdict.

The specification

Not a proposal for an ontology. An ontology.

02

Document 02 is a normative specification with machine-readable companions: an OWL ontology extending CIDOC-CRM 7.1.3 (ISO 21127), a governed relation registry, SHACL constraints carrying the integrity rules, and one event modelled end to end. It parses to 1,804 triples and the worked example validates without violation.

marsad.ttlClasses, properties, controlled vocabularies. CIDOC-CRM for events, people and places; LRMoo for the work → recension → edition → witness chain; PROV-O for extraction provenance; OWL-Time for interval relations.
relations.ttlThe relation registry, with inverses, symmetry, and two restricted types — naskh and asbāb al-nuzūl — that no extraction pipeline may ever assert.
shapes.ttlTwelve integrity invariants as enforced constraints rather than editorial guidance, because editorial guidance erodes.
thawr.ttlThe shelter at Ghār Thawr: one event, one chain, one grading, one unresolved dispute, and one route segment that admits it is guessing.

Three gaps CIDOC-CRM does not cover

The standard has no concept of a transmitted report whose chain of custody is itself the object of scholarly assessment. That is not an oversight — it is a genuine structural feature of Islamic historiography with no close equivalent in the European museum tradition CRM was built around. Three new classes fill it: mrs:Report, mrs:TransmissionChain, mrs:Relationship. They are the ontology's substantive contribution and the place reviewer attention should concentrate.

What the model refuses to do

This is the fragment worth reading, from the worked example. Five narrator links; for each, the system computes whether a meeting was chronologically and geographically plausible — the classical لقاء liqāʾ check, run across the whole corpus continuously.

Twenty places where a specialist is required

The specification marks every decision it deliberately declines to make — ⟨scholar⟩ — and forbids an implementation from resolving one automatically. Among them: whether a chain is continuous; whether a person's companion status is established; whether two names denote one transmitter or two; which calendar-conversion convention holds for a region and period; whether inclusion in a collection constitutes an explicit grading; and the direction of a mawlā tie, which the sources frequently leave ambiguous and which must never be guessed from name order.

Those twenty marks are the shape of the invitation. They are not gaps to be closed by better software — several of them cannot be closed by software at all. They are the specification stating, in advance and in writing, exactly where it needs historians, and committing that what a historian decides there is recorded under their name, permanently, and remains revisable by whoever comes after them.

Nūr and Nujūm

A visual grammar that is also a data encoding — and resolves the depiction problem structurally.

05

The guiding image for this project is that the Prophet ﷺ is nūr and his Companions are nujūm. Treated not as decoration but as the formal basis of the visual language, it happens to resolve three competing requirements at once: it honours the prohibition on depiction absolutely, it satisfies “do not reduce him to one node among millions” structurally rather than by enlarging a dot, and it encodes real, sourced data — generational distance in transmission.

The morph field

How four dimensions interact instead of occupying four tabs.

06

Every entity carries several stable coordinate assignments at once, computed offline. The rendered position is a weighted blend of them, and the weights are a continuous control. Pull toward geography and the network drifts onto the map, people sliding to the cities they lived in. Pull toward time and the same population reorganizes into generations.

The enabling decision is unglamorous. Positions are computed once, globally, offline, and never move during interaction. That converts the hardest engineering problem in the project — layout stability across time-scrubbing, filtering and sharing — into a batch job, and it makes position mean something, every time.

Technology, chosen last

Requirements first. The least fashionable decision is the load-bearing one.

07

Ten storage requirements were derived before any product was named — immutable audit, bitemporality, transactional integrity, deep traversal, geospatial indexing, Arabic full-text, vector search, binary storage, bulk export, zero lock-in. No single technology satisfies them. Any architecture claiming otherwise is selling something.

Why PostgreSQL is the centre, and not a graph database

A graph database is better at traversal and worse at everything else this project needs. Traversal is one requirement out of ten; making the traversal engine the system of record subordinates nine requirements to one. Postgres provides ACID guarantees, native range types for temporal modelling, the best open geospatial implementation in existence via PostGIS, vectors via pgvector, and an operational record measured in decades. Graph traversal is added as a projection when latency evidence demands it — at which point the choice of engine is reversible, because it owns nothing. Neo4j's demonstrated ceiling is far above this project's needs, so there is no scale-driven urgency to adopt early.

Blender is a build-time asset factory, never a source of truth and never in the runtime path. The test for any Blender output: if the corpus changes, does this asset regenerate automatically? If not, it is hand-authored content masquerading as data, and it will drift out of agreement with the evidence in the most visible part of the product.

Gate on 3D L2 may begin only when a specific analytical question has been identified that two dimensions genuinely cannot answer. Without that gate, 3D arrives because it is exciting, and the project acquires a demo it must maintain forever.

The honest reading of this table. The last row is not novel because every cell is filled. It is novel because of what fills the fifth column. Several systems have a source apparatus — Seshat has uncertainty with citations; Six Degrees of Francis Bacon distinguishes documented from inferred relationships. None has one in which the report with its chain is the primitive and everything else is derived from it.

What is genuinely novel — and what is not

10
  1. The report-first data modelNo surveyed system derives events, biographies and relationships from reports-with-chains. Every one stores facts and attaches sources.The strongest claim, and the one thing that cannot be retrofitted into any existing system
  2. The isnād as a four-fold objectEvidentiary chain, social network, geographic diffusion path and temporal ordering, in one encoding.Others have each half; nobody has fused them
  3. Historiographic time as a navigable axisHolding the event fixed and moving the record — watching the tradition itself form across the 1st–3rd centuries AH.A research capability, not a presentational feature
  4. The morph fieldContinuous interpolation between network, geographic, temporal and transmission space in one persistent scene.Palladio coordinates these views; this integrates them
  5. Tradition-scoped attributed gradingGrades as acts by named evaluators, never normalized across incommensurable epistemic systems.The question that matters in this domain is whose judgement
  6. The citable ViewStateA configuration of evidence as a version-pinned, resolvable scholarly citation.Makes visualization citable, AI answers auditable, lessons authorable and accessibility structural — from one object

Not novel, and not to be claimed: knowledge graphs for history; isnād network visualization (Hadith-Viz and the SNA literature were there first); historical gazetteers (Pelagios, Pleiades, al-Ṯurayyā); uncertainty in historical networks (Six Degrees of Francis Bacon); time-sliders over historical maps; LLMs over Islamic texts; 4D historical reconstruction as an ambition (Venice Time Machine). The novelty is the composition and the inversion, not the components — and saying so plainly is more persuasive to the scholarly audience whose participation the project requires, because that audience knows the prior art better than the builders do.

Buildability

Assessed against September 2026 technology.

12

Possible today

  • The complete data model — reports, chains, reified relationships, tri-temporal extents, uncertainty geometry, attributed evaluations, disputes, coverage gaps
  • Append-only signed log with derived projections
  • Temporal constraint solving with uncertainty propagation
  • Historical GIS with per-segment route confidence
  • L0 and L1 in full, including the morph field
  • WebGPU rendering of 10⁵–10⁶ elements
  • The AI navigation layer exactly as specified
  • Isnād/matn segmentation and chain parsing at corpus scale

Possible with significant engineering

  • Layout stability anchored across corpus versions as the corpus grows by orders of magnitude
  • Arabic retrieval that feels intelligent rather than adequate
  • Full L2/L3 at 60fps on mid-range mobile
  • Federation across independently governed institutional nodes
  • Review tooling efficient enough that scholarship is not the sole constraint

Experimental / emerging

  • Narrator disambiguation at corpus scale — an open research problem, and the project's hardest
  • Reliable relationship extraction from classical Arabic prose
  • Bitemporal graph databases (mature relationally, not in graph)
  • Uncertainty encodings validated to be read correctly by non-specialists — an empirical question, not a technical one

Future, or never appropriate

  • Fully automated extraction trustworthy enough to bypass review — and this is not a goal. Human adjudication is not a temporary limitation to engineer away; it is the source of the corpus's authority
  • Machine translation of transmitted material — prohibited on policy grounds regardless of capability
  • Automated adjudication of scholarly disagreement — permanently outside the remit

Nothing in the core system requires a technology that does not exist. The project is not blocked on research. Its difficulty is the discipline to model evidence first, the institutional capacity to sustain scholarly review for decades, and the restraint to keep the visualization subordinate to the corpus.

Self-critique

A conventional first design was drafted, then attacked. Thirty weaknesses; six were structural.

11

The items below could not have been fixed later — they would have required rebuilding the corpus. They are why this exercise belongs before the code rather than after it. The full thirty, and three that remain unresolved, are in the corpus.

  1. Facts were primary; sources were attachedThe standard model, and unrepairable: a corpus authored as settled statements cannot have its evidence reconstructed.Inverted the model — the report became the primitive
  2. Grading was a property of a reportreport.grade = "sahih" is a category error. Grading is contested, evaluator-dependent and historically situated.Grades became acts by named authorities; conflicting gradings now coexist natively
  3. Confidence was a single floatA normalized score spanning Sunni ḥadīth criticism, Shia rijāl and academic source criticism is a fabrication with a decimal point.Derived bands within a declared tradition lens, never normalized across incommensurable systems
  4. One normative account of contested historyWorkable for the Seerah; for the period after 11 AH it silently encodes one tradition's account as truth.Added the interpretation dimension and tradition lenses; divergence modelled as attributed disputes
  5. Only one time axisEvent-time alone discards when a claim was recorded — among the most informative variables available.Added record-time, which produced an unanticipated capability now among the strongest novelty claims
  6. Dates stored as timestamps; routes as lines; places as coordinatesEach manufactures precision the sources do not support, invisibly and irreversibly. A line from Makkah to Madinah erases the entire historical content of the Hijrah.Four-corner intervals, segmented routes with per-segment confidence, places as named concepts with time-bounded geometry states

Three that remain unresolved

Stated here rather than left for critics to find. How to present contested material at the Explore lens — plain-language hedging is reasonable but untested with real children and teachers. Whether non-specialists read uncertainty encoding correctly — the entire honesty architecture rests on an empirical claim about perception that the project asserts and has not tested; if it is false, the architecture is sound and the product still misleads. Cross-tradition representation without false equivalence — the mechanisms exist, but the editorial judgement is irreducibly human and will be contested however carefully it is exercised.

Sequence

The corpus must be embarrassing to look at before it is impressive.

09
Q1Ontology and foundations. Board convened before schema is written. CIDOC-CRM-aligned ontology, append-only log, temporal and spatial types, licence register.
Q2The Hijrah corpus. 60–120 persons, 300–800 reports, chains parsed, routes graded, disputes documented — all scholar-reviewed. L0 reader tier live before any visualization exists.
Q3The observatory. Network, timeline and map; stable precomputed layout; the morph field; the evidence ladder; time-scrubbing with uncertainty.
Q4Navigation and public beta. Typed-query AI, binding validator, refusal paths, citable ViewState, published adversarial-test results.
Year 2The full Seerah at the Hijrah's evidentiary standard. Scholar tooling. Multi-tradition lenses. First DOI-assigned open data release.
Year 3The Companions and first two generations. Transmission layer at scale. 3D only if the gate is met. First peer-reviewed publication citing a pinned view.
Years 4–6Federation — other institutions run their own nodes under their own scholarly authority. The point at which the project stops depending on its founders.
Years 7–10The citation substrate. Continued existence independent of any individual, organization or funder — the definition of infrastructure, and the only outcome that justifies the ambition.
Budget shape ~40% engineering, 30% scholarly labour, 15% rights and acquisition, 15% infrastructure. If scholarly labour drops below a quarter, the project has quietly become a visualization company.

Kill criteria, stated in advance

Projects of this ambition rarely fail loudly — they drift, absorb funding, and are quietly maintained into irrelevance. Stop or restructure if: scholarly participation does not materialize within eighteen months; the corpus repeatedly fails adversarial review; a funding cycle passes in which the corpus grows less than the interface; governance cannot achieve cross-tradition representation; or adversarial testing continues to elicit fabricated transmitted material — in which case ship without the AI layer, which the architecture permits by design.

Red lines

Any of these, if crossed, would justify halting rather than shipping.

  • No AI-generated ḥadīth, or AI text presented as transmitted material
  • No AI grading of authenticity
  • No figurative depiction of the Prophet ﷺ, his family, or the Companions — in any tier, medium or context, including XR
  • No fatwa, ruling or doctrinal determination issued by the system
  • No computed ranking of persons by religious significance
  • No presentation of a contested matter as settled
  • No fabricated precision in a date, a location, a route or a chain
  • No silent modification of the record
  • No paywall on evidence — provenance is never a premium feature
  • No visualization whose warrant cannot be inspected

The Islamic scholarly tradition spent centuries building an apparatus for answering one question with more rigour than any comparable tradition achieved: how do we know this, and from whom?

The opportunity is not to visualize Islamic history. Others have done that, some well. It is to build the first system whose data model is the tradition's own epistemology — so that the structure always present in the sources, but observable only to those who had read tens of thousands of pages, becomes observable to anyone, without becoming any less true.

An observatory does not create the sky. It makes an existing, vast, structurally invisible reality observable — and its worth depends entirely on the honesty of its instruments.
