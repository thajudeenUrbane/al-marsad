    Line style: kind of relationship

      Light: generation, and nothing else

      • The Prophet ﷺ: the light at the centre, the source of the light, not one mark among many
      • Companion (ṣaḥābī): brightest and coolest, nearest the light
      • Successor (tābiʿī): warmer and dimmer, further out
      • Successor of the Successors: warmer and dimmer still
      • Generation not recorded: plain, with no halo; placed by its links only

      Brightness and warmth show the recorded generation (ṭabaqa) only: never importance, never how many links a person has. Switch it off with “Light by generation”.

      What else the Sky shows, and does not

      • Not yet reviewed: every claim today. Each row of the panel carries its status label.
      • Size: off. No mark is sized by any measure, so nothing reads as rank.
      • Position: in the Sky, distance from the light is the recorded generation; everything else sits by its recorded links. In the flat views, the view's own axis. Dimmed marks are outside the focus, thread or period; hollow marks have no recorded date.
      • Motion: only when you turn, zoom, flick or change the view, or press Play history or Orbit. Nothing drifts on its own.
      • Every edge is sharp: nothing is blurred, so every star can be told apart and touched.
      • The faint pinpoints far behind are scenery, not records. Switch them off with “Distant stars”.
      • Geography has no basemap yet: only a grid of degrees and the places whose coordinates are recorded.
