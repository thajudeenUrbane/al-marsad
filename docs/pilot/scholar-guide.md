al-Marṣad: a guide for reviewers (pilot)
=========================================

Assalāmu ʿalaykum, and thank you for helping.

WHAT THIS IS
al-Marṣad records what the sources say about the Seerah, one claim at a
time, and who checked each one. Nothing is accepted until a reviewer
accepts it. Software never accepts a claim on its own.

The first batch is from the "50 Stories" document. An AI platform wrote
those stories, and nobody knows yet whether they are accurate. Please
treat every story claim as unchecked. To ACCEPT one, you must name a real
source with its place in the book, for example:
    Ibn Hishām, al-Sīra al-Nabawiyya, vol. 1 p. 480
The page will not let you accept a story claim without a source.

The second batch asks "same person?" or "is this a real group?" about
entries in the graph.

HOW TO REVIEW (phone or laptop)
1. Open the link (or the file) you were sent. Enter your reviewer ID
   when asked. You are given the ID; your name is never asked for.
2. Each card shows one claim, with its source text beside or below it.
3. Tick the checks you made:
     - the report is in the source
     - the chain is sound
     - the summary is fair
     - the people are identified correctly
4. Choose one:
     ACCEPT            (a source is needed for a story claim)
     REJECT
     NEEDS DISCUSSION  (please write a short note; it goes to the board)
5. Your work is saved on your device as you go. You can stop and come
   back later, even without internet.
6. When you are done, tap SEND MY DECISIONS. It makes one small file.
   Send it on WhatsApp (or email) to the maintainer. Sending it twice is
   safe.

On a laptop: 1-4 tick the checks; A accept, R reject, D discuss;
J and K move between claims; S goes to Send.

PLEASE
- Do not write anyone's name, phone number or email in notes.
- If you are unsure, choose NEEDS DISCUSSION and say why.
- If you change your mind after sending, tell the maintainer. A change
  after sending goes through the board.

Publicly, decisions are credited to the review board, not to you by
name, unless you agree to be named later.

Jazākum Allāhu khayran.
