# Slice 2c-site handover: three doors, the hub, the thread, plain words, experiments

Spec: docs/handover/slice-2-brief.md, 2c items 1, 4, 5 and 6, plus the hub. No data changed, no status upgraded, no name, date or definition invented. Nothing needs an account.

## What exists (site/)
- **Home = three doors + hub** (R4, R9). Discover → the stories in reading order (story first) and the first story. Study → `study/`: events in time order (by earliest recorded day, date shown as recorded, undated ones apart), then people and groups. Research → `research/`: how far the record is checked, who recorded the claims and what works they cite (the 50 Stories marked AI-generated), records with open questions or gaps, downloads (`data/graph.ttl`; `data/external-ids.csv` when it exists), every record with its Turtle. Every Study/Research link opens the entity page at `#evidence`: one page per entity, one URL, works with JS off.
- **#evidence** on every entity page: a section (h2 "Evidence", lead naming the record) wrapping the claim sections (now h3) and open questions/gaps, after the summary (and after the story text on story pages).
- **Hub** (`hub.py`): views other builders write into site/dist — observatory, timeline, lifelines, map, learn, ask. The home page (and the Study/Research door lists) link a view only if `<folder>/index.html` exists. `site/build.py` never deletes those folders, so either order works: view builders then `site/build.py`; or `site/build.py`, view builders, then `site/build.py --hub` (rewrites only home, study, research). check_all.sh does the second; `site/check_hub.py` fails if a built view is not linked or a linked one is missing. New view: one line in `hub.VIEWS` + `hub.view.<folder>.name/.desc` in en.json. With main of 15 Oct: observatory, timeline, map, learn linked; lifelines and ask appear once their builders run before the `--hub` line.
- **Thread view** (`thread.py` → `thread.json` 57.8 KB; `static/thread.js` 13 KB), port of generation-1/wider-network: on person and group pages a hidden switch (shown only by JS) opens the thread: dated events in order, then stories in reading order (no story has a date; never placed in time), undated events, ties to people/groups, places. Each moment: date/place, title link, each claim as a short sentence with role, **status** and source, "Also here" chips. A chip follows the thread in place (path shown, Back/Forward work, `#thread=person/<id>` shareable, Escape/Close returns). Every string comes from en.json via thread.json or the page.
- **Plain words** (B11; `static/enhance.js` 2.3 KB on every page, switch in the header, hidden without JS): hides an Arabic term in brackets after its English ("Generation (ṭabaqa)", "the Trench (al-Khandaq)") and shows a glossary-recorded plain wording in place of free-standing terms. Wordings live in glossary.json (`plain` + `plain_source`), taken only where the repo pairs them, test-checked: ṭabaqa→generation, Nasab→patronymic chain, Khandaq→the Trench, Ridda→apostasy. Choice kept in localStorage (try/catch).
- **Experiments** (`experiments.py`, `experiments/`): the demo, generation-1 (index, at-scale, wider-network) and stories-50 copied as earlier prototypes, wrapped as full documents, web-font links removed (any other outside load fails the build), a note on top and the licence footer at the end. Originals untouched. check_html checks the copies for tags/links/footer, not reader layout.
- **External IDs** (`external.py`): reads `ontology/corpus/external-ids.csv` (slice 5 format) **when present** — not on main yet. Details layer: "Possible matches elsewhere: Wikidata Q… (named there …) proposed, not reviewed", tool's evidence and rating as notes; only Wikidata gets a link (ID pattern checked). Tested with a temp CSV; prefixes test-checked against identity_audit.
- `render.Renderer` gained an optional `external=`; `shell()` now adds the footer template, the plain-words switch and enhance.js to every page it builds (the interactive pages use it too; their checks pass).

2. thread.json is one shared file (58 KB, gzips well); if the corpus grows ~4×, split it per kind or per entity.
3. Thread lines repeat claims recorded twice (an assertion and a participation for the same event); both are shown, each with its status.
4. The experiments copies are served on the public site; delete `experiments.PROTOTYPES` rows to stop serving one.
5. The hub's one-line descriptions of other agents' views are mine (en.json `hub.view.*`); check them against their handovers.
