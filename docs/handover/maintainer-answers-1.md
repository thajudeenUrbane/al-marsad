# Maintainer answers, round 1 — 15 October 2026

Product decisions from the maintainer's answers to the founding questions (95 questions; the answer sheet is a private artifact). Personal answers are left out of the repo. Where an answer conflicts with an earlier doc, the answer wins and the conflict is noted.

## Purpose and audience
- Accuracy first: when daʿwa, education and scholarship conflict, **scholarship wins**.
- Primary reader: **serious students and researchers**; everyone welcome, Muslims first. First visitors: academic contacts.
- Wanted: versions per level of reader, and a **"plain words" on/off option** for easier terminology (fits doc 13 D1 depths).
- Long term: founder hands over to a **scholarly body**. Founder decides product; scholars decide content.
- Year-one scope: **the Seerah and the Companions' generation**; "Seerah first, decide later" on the 1,400-year scope.

## Scholars and review (sizes slice 3)
- 2–3 historical scholars now, 3–6 h/week each, working on **phone and laptop**.
- They check all four: report exists in source, chain soundness, summary fairness, identity of people.
- Reviewers are **named on every claim** they review. Disagreement: **the board votes**. Mostly volunteers; a small stipend for some.
- Withdrawn endorsement: the board decides. Sectarian criticism: scholars respond.

## Traditions and sensitivity
- **One board first** (Sunni, across the four madhhabs, coordinated initially by Shāfiʿī scholars); others later. This narrows doc 06 §9 principle 1 (cross-tradition board) for the first release and must be stated publicly as a declared default.
- Shīʿī narratives: out of scope for now. Conflict among the Companions (the fitna, Jamal, Ṣiffīn): avoid for now.
- Weak reports: show only famous ones, to correct them. Poorly sourced popular stories: show with an honest grade.
- Honorifics on **every mention**.

## Sources
- First ingestion source: **Ibn Hishām's Sīra**; edition chosen by the scholars; English translated in-house with scholar review.
- Trusted works: classical sources, plus *Sīrat Sayyid al-Bashar* by Kodambuzha Bava Musliyar.
- **The 50 Stories document was generated by an AI platform** and its authenticity is unknown. It must be labelled as AI-drafted and reviewed by scholars soon. (Corrects the slice 1c provenance, which credited it as the maintainer's compilation.)

- Possibly-same people: show nothing until a scholar rules.
- Groups and tribes: **full pages**. The Prophet ﷺ: **a distinct, respectful page**.
- Home: graph-first; each page **story first, chain one tap below**.

- Accounts later, depth-dependent (basic for public, fuller for researchers). Reading never needs one (doc 13 D1).
