# Maintainer answers, round 2 — 15 October 2026

Follow-ups to round 1 (docs/handover/maintainer-answers-1.md), answered on the same private answer sheet. Where round 2 changes round 1, round 2 wins.

## Scholars' review tool (slice 3)
- **R1 Language:** the tool is in English, with the **Arabic source text beside each claim** wherever the graph has it.
- **R2 Sending decisions:** WhatsApp is fine for sending decision files.
- **R3 Naming (changes round 1 C18):** "Only name the board. No need to mention scholars now. But keep it as recorded." Publicly, reviews are credited to the review board, and individual scholars are not named. Who reviewed what is still recorded. Because all data in this repo is public, reviewer names live only in a private roster outside the repo, and the public data carries opaque reviewer IDs. A scholar can be named later if they agree.

## The public site (slice 2)
- **R4 Home page:** one home with three doors: **Discover** (stories), **Study** (people, timelines), **Research** (sources and data). Replaces the graph-first home of round 1 B10.
- **R9 Order by depth:** Discover shows the story first, with the chain one tap below. Study and Research show the chain (the evidence) first.
- **R8 Status in screenshots:** a clear text label on every claim, and the same label printed into any image someone shares (a requirement for any future share-image feature).
- **R5 Accounts (needs clarification):** "No, deeper levels need an account." This conflicts with two standing rules: doc 13 D1 ("Reading needs no account") and red line 9 in docs/06 ("No paywall on evidence. The provenance of a claim is never a premium feature."). Asked again: should accounts gate deeper *evidence*, or only deeper *tools* (saved views, notes, research workspace)? Until answered, nothing behind a login is built. Slices 2 and 3 have no accounts.

## Scope and content
- **R6 The fitna period** (Jamal and Ṣiffīn, 36–37 AH): cover later, once the board exists.
- **R7 Young learners:** the young-learner mode is for learners aged about 15 and up. Still later, after the reader pages.
- **R11 *Seerathu Sayyidil Bashar*** by Kodampuzha Bava Musliyar: in the maintainer's words, a prominent biography of the Prophet ﷺ and "a reference book by recent years". Treat it as a modern reference work, not a classical source; the scholars decide how it is used.
- **R10 Name:** keep *al-Marṣad* for now.
