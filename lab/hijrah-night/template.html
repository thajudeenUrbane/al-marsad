    الهجرة

    Hijrah Night

    Walk the Hijrah over the real land of the Ḥijāz, from Makkah to Madinah, at night. The Prophet ﷺ is shown only as a light. Everyone else is a smaller light, and nobody is drawn as a figure.

    Every line you read about the past comes from Ibn Hishām's Sīra, with its volume and page. Sometimes you will be asked to guess before you read, because trying to remember first is how memory works. When the text disagrees with itself, you will see both reports.

    A lab prototype. Claims were extracted from the Arabic text by AI and have not yet been reviewed by scholars. Drag to look around, scroll or pinch to zoom, and double-click the land to move there.

    How this works

    • Every statement about the past is a claim: an English gloss of one claim extracted from the Arabic of Ibn Hishām's Sīra (OpenITI text, Cairo edition of 1955, provisional), with the volume, page and OpenITI milestone it rests on. Tap a citation to see the claim itself.
    • Written by the editor: chapter titles, which sections each chapter draws on, where the camera looks, and the questions. None of these assert anything.
    • Answers come from claims. When you are wrong, the reason shown is another claim, for example that the text records ʿUmar emigrating earlier.
    • Places are drawn only where a gazetteer gives a point: al-Thurayya (CC BY 4.0) and Wikidata (CC0). No path is drawn between stops, because the text gives their order, not the road.
    • People are lights. The Prophet ﷺ is the warm light, never a figure. Those named at the council are drawn as grey stones, neither stars nor light; whether and when each later accepted Islam is not drawn until recorded claims and the scholars decide.
    • Your sky is kept only in this browser. Each person you meet becomes a star; each chapter becomes a constellation. Star positions are decorative, not astronomical.
    • Depths: Discover shows the story; Study adds chains of narration, family links and the sections used; Research adds claim IDs and character spans.
