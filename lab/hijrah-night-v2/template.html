    الهجرة

    Hijrah Night

    The Hijrah, step by step: from the years of pressure in Makkah, through the pledges at ʿAqaba and the emigrants who went ahead, to the plot, the night of leaving, three nights in the cave, the road north, the arrival at Qubāʾ and the entry into Madinah.

    Every statement carries its evidence: Established by the Qurʾān, authenticated ḥadīth or agreement of the sources; Supported by an early narration; Disputed where reports differ or a chain is weak; Reconstruction for the land and the light only, never for what anyone did or said.

    A draft for scholars to review. The narrative was drafted with AI from the Arabic of Ibn Hishām and other named sources (round 3, G3: Claude drafts, a scholar edits and approves). The Prophet ﷺ appears only as a light; nobody is drawn as a figure. Drag to look, scroll or pinch to zoom.

    How to read this

    • Evidence grades. Established needs the Qurʾān, an authenticated ḥadīth, or agreement of independent early sources. Supported means an early source narrates it and nothing contradicts it. Disputed always says why: different reports, a weak chain, or a later popular tradition. Reconstruction is only the environment (terrain, light, distance) and states its basis.
    • Sources. Ibn Hishām's Sīra by section, with volume and page (Cairo edition of 1955, provisional). Qurʾān references link to the verse; the short meaning shown is in our own words, not a translation. Ḥadīth and other references marked "to verify" wait for a scholar to check the wording and numbering.
    • In the graph. Each stage lists the AI-extracted claims of the sections it rests on, so the narrative can be checked against the data.
    • The world. Places are drawn only where a gazetteer locates them, with their certainty; unlocated places are dashed and nothing is drawn for them. No road is drawn between stops. The sky follows the time of day the sources give, or is marked as reconstruction.
    • People. The Prophet ﷺ is the warm light. Others are lights; gatherings of believers are clusters of light; people, tribes and crowds named without a recorded side are grey stones; only groups acting against the Prophet ﷺ in that moment, such as the watchers at the door or the trackers, are dark stones. Nobody is depicted.
    • Depth. Discover hides source chips; Study shows them; Research adds claim IDs.
    • Your sky keeps the people you meet, only in this browser.
