# Consent and assent — Hijrah journey pilot

## Letter and consent form for parents and guardians

Dear parent or guardian,

This month our class will try a new way of learning about the Hijrah, the journey of the Prophet Muḥammad ﷺ from Makkah to Madinah.
For one half of the story your child will use an interactive map of the journey on a computer; for the other half they will read the same
information on a plain web page. We want to find out which way helps children **remember** more, a week and a month later.

**What your child will do**
- Answer a short quiz before and after the lesson, then again one week later and one month later (about 15 minutes each time).
- Spend about 50 minutes in one lesson using the map and the reading page.
- The quizzes are **not marked** and do not count towards any grades or reports.

**What we collect**
- A code instead of your child’s name (for example “P07”), their age in years, which half they used on the map, and their quiz answers and scores.
- We do **not** record audio, video or screens, and we do not collect names, addresses, photos or dates of birth in the results.
- Only the teacher keeps the list linking names to codes, on paper and locked away. It is destroyed when the last quiz is scored.

**Who sees it**
Only the class teacher and the people helping to mark the quizzes. If we share what we learn, it will be as totals for the whole class,
so no child can be identified.

**Your choices**
Taking part is voluntary. If you say no, or your child says no, they will still have the lesson as usual and nothing else changes.
You or your child can stop at any time without giving a reason. Ask us to delete your child’s answers at any point until one month after the last quiz,
after which the results no longer link to any child.

**Questions:** please contact the class teacher. If you have a concern about how this is run, contact the headteacher or the school’s safeguarding lead.

## Assent — for the child (read aloud with the teacher)

We are trying two ways of learning the story of the Hijrah: a map you can explore on a computer, and a page you can read.
We want to know which one helps people remember more.

If you join in:
- you will answer some short quizzes — today, in one week and in one month;
- the quizzes are **not** tests for marks. Nobody will be told off for not knowing;
- we won’t write your name on the results, only a code;
- nothing is recorded — no video, no sound, no screenshots.

## Data handling summary (for staff)
| Item | Rule |
|---|---|
| Identifiers | Random code only in all data; name–code list on paper, locked, destroyed after the 1-month test is scored |
| Collected | Code, class, age (years), group, form order, session dates, item scores, “studied in between?” yes/no |
| Not collected | Names, dates of birth, photos, recordings, screen captures, device analytics |
| Storage | School-managed storage only; paper tests locked; shred after 12 months |
| Withdrawal | At any time; delete on request until anonymisation (1 month after last test) |
| Sharing | Class-level totals only |
