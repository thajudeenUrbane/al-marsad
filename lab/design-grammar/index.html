The aniconic design grammar المرصد

Every mark in al-Marṣad's night world, what it means, what licenses it, and what it may never say. No person is ever drawn as a figure: people are light, name, relation and sound of narration about them. The Prophet ﷺ is the light at the centre, never a node.

Specimen for GRAMMAR.md v0.1 · draft for the scholars' board · 17 Oct 2026 · the stars image is our own metaphor, never a hadith

  • No pixel without provenanceEvery data mark opens its source. Decoration never varies with data.
  • Aniconism is absoluteNo face, body, silhouette, hand or avatar, for anyone, in any medium.
  • AI never assertsA mark licensed only by a model output is never drawn.
  • Computed ≠ significantCounts are coverage of the record, never rank. Brightness is generation only.
  • Uncertainty is drawnCertainty rings, dispute rings and absence, never silent filling.

The vocabulary

Each card shows the mark at 1× with its rule. Motion follows the reduced-motion setting of your device.

The light النور

The Prophet ﷺ: the source of the light the others receive. Shown beside his name, never as a shape.

Encodes
recorded presence at a place and time
Licence
a located, dated claim per station; otherwise it waits at an absence mark
Never
a node, count, rank, tooltip score, figure, footprint or voice
Spec
--light 28 px core, 160 px halo, breath 6 s ±6 %

The lights الأنوار

People who accepted Islam. Brightness is generation (T4), never worth. Same colour and size for women and men.

Encodes
presence in the record; generation; scholar-accepted companionship
Licence
a sourced assertion naming the person; companionship accepted by a scholar
Never
virtue, rank, Paradise, size by computed degree (outside a labelled coverage view)
Spec
--person 6 px core; opacity 1 / .72 / .52 / .36

Stones الحجارة

Recorded opposition, at this time, according to these sources (X1). Turns to light only at a recorded, sourced acceptance of Islam.

Encodes
sourced rel_opposed / rel_fought_against within its time span
Licence
opposition claim; for the turn, a dated acceptance claim (relation type still to be added)
Never
a verdict on final state, tribe, "evil", red, a figure; children are never stones
Spec
8×7 px heptagon, --stone-fill, 1 px --stone, no halo; turn 2.4 s

Absence الغياب

What the record does not give: honest, empty, and documented. Never filled by guess, model or reconstruction.

Encodes
lc_unlocated, undated, unnamed, cs_stub, mrs:CoverageGap
Licence
a source that mentions the thing without placing or naming it, or a coverage record
Never
interpolated positions, dates or straight-line routes
Spec
1 px dashed --absence, dash 3/3, empty

Certainty rings حلقات اليقين

The reviewed certainty of the claim behind a mark. Rejected and superseded claims are not drawn.

Encodes
lc_*, as_proposed, as_under_review, mrs:Dispute
Licence
the claim's own status; disputes list every position with its source
Never
hadith grade, narrator reliability, a scholar's view of a person
Spec
rings at core +4 / +8 px; dispute outer ring --dispute with notch

Chains الإسناد

The recorded chain of a specific report, rising link by link. Marfūʿ chains end in the light's halo, not at a node.

Encodes
TransmissionChain, TransmissionLink, ContactCheck, ascription label
Licence
the chain as the collection gives it
Never
a grade we computed; line weight by "strength"
Spec
--chain 1 px; dashed if unverified; gap = absence mark; 600 ms / link

Constellations الكوكبات

Recorded co-participation in a recorded event, appearing once the story has passed through it, and remaining as memory.

Encodes
mrs:Participation at both ends of each line
Licence
reviewed participant list; 50 Stories list allowed with an "unreviewed list" note (E3)
Never
computed clusters, factions, similarity (research views only, labelled)
Spec
--constellation 1 px; label Arabic + Latin

Calligraphy الخط

Names beside the light, in the hilye spirit: words describe, nothing depicts.

Encodes
name, kunya, nasab, laqab, honorific, each sourced
Licence
a source for each name form; commissioned panels credited
Never
letters arranged into a figure; generated calligraphy; guessed vocalisation
Spec
--f-arabic 1.15–1.25× Latin, line-height 1.8, no letter-spacing

Time and season الزمان

The sky turns with the lunar calendar (T2). Moon phase only for a recorded date, labelled as computed.

Encodes
recorded dates and spans; seasons only where a source states them
Licence
a dated claim; undated events sit in a span with an absence edge
Never
a precise hour or day not in the sources

Weather الجو

Atmosphere, never theatre. With no source, the sky is clear and neutral.

Encodes
weather a source describes
Licence
an accepted passage, quoted in the panel
Never
divine favour or anger as an effect; angels or miracles as images (text only)

Sound الصوت

Voice and ambience. No instruments (the duff is the board's question). See SOUND.md for policies A, B, C.

Encodes
place, recitation, narration about people
Licence
credited recording; sourced passage
Never
the Prophet ﷺ voiced; Companions in the first person; battle sound

Decoration

Background stars and horizon. Never interactive, never varies with data, never in the light or person hues.

Encodes
nothing
Spec
--faint < .5 opacity; the only marks allowed to twinkle

Forbidden

  • Any figure, face, silhouette, shadow, hand, avatar or generated image of the Prophet ﷺ, his family, the Companions, the prophets or angels
  • First-person camera or body in XR for any of them
  • A voice for the Prophet ﷺ; Companions voiced in the first person
  • The central light as a node, count or rank
  • The stars metaphor quoted as hadith
  • Size, glow or colour encoding virtue, rank or Paradise
  • Gendered colours or hidden marks for women
  • Interpolated places, dates or straight-line routes
  • Battle spectacle: blood, weapons, impacts, kill counts
  • Musical instruments (duff only if the board allows)
  • Any mark licensed only by a model output
  • Points, badges or "unlocking" tied to sacred persons
