# Partner letters: drafts

Drafts for the maintainer to edit and send personally. Nothing here has been sent. Each letter is under 250 words, makes one concrete ask, and says what we give back.

**Before sending**
- Fill every `[bracket]`. Find the right contact on each organisation's own website or through a personal introduction. No names or addresses are guessed here.
- Check each organisation's current programmes on its own site first; details below come from public sources read in October 2026 and may have changed.
- Attach or link the Hijrah Night preview only once the private link works (P2), and say plainly that it is unreviewed.
- Send one letter at a time. Note replies in a private log, not in the repo.

**Shared facts used in the letters** (keep consistent)
- al-Marṣad: an open, evidence-anchored knowledge graph of the Sīra, starting with Ibn Hishām via OpenITI. Every claim points to an exact passage (volume, page, section).
- Scholar review per claim; public pages show only accepted content after review.
- Non-profit and donation-funded. Code AGPL, data CC BY-SA, identifiers CC0.
- Prototype: Hijrah Night, ten chapters of the Hijrah over real terrain, built from 277 claims in 28 sections. Every sentence a learner reads is a claim with its page.
- The companion "points, never speaks": it chooses claims, and the page shows them. It cannot assert anything on its own.
