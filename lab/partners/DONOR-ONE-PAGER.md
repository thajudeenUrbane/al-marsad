# al-Marṣad: the life of the Prophet ﷺ, with the source behind every word

*Draft one-pager for donors. The maintainer edits and sends it personally. Figures are planning estimates, not quotes.*

## What it is
al-Marṣad is an open, non-profit map of the Sīra. We have read the whole of Ibn Hishām's *Sīra* into a knowledge graph of people, places, events and relations. Every statement points to the exact passage it comes from, and scholars review it claim by claim. Next we are building **worlds**: places and journeys you can move through, starting with the Hijrah, where every sentence carries its source and where reports differ, both are shown.

## Why now
- **Many people now ask AI chatbots about the Sīra**, and the answers rarely show where they come from. We are building the opposite: a companion that can only point to recorded reports and cannot make anything up.
- **The Hijrah route is being restored** from Thawr to Qubāʾ, and many visitors will walk it. None of them yet has a sourced companion.
- **The tools are finally cheap.** The site runs for under $600 a year in hosting. Almost every dollar given goes to people and scholarship.

## Proof, not promises
*Hijrah Night* is a working prototype: ten chapters of the journey over real terrain, built from 277 claims in 28 sections of Ibn Hishām. The story arc comes from the sources, with no invented dialogue and no depiction. Where the location of a stop is unknown, it is shown as unknown. [Link to preview, when public.]
